The problem

Right now, the design waits.

The yard gets measured by hand. The proposal gets built later, back at a desk, from photos and memory. By the time it comes back, the moment of belief is gone.

Hours of setup Disconnected tools Stitched together after the visit

What we build

One visit, start to finish.

Imaginova turns the traditional backyard sales process into a single connected experience: accurate site capture, design exploration, customer collaboration, and a proposal, instead of disconnected tools stitched together after the fact.

01 · Scan

Scan the real yard.

The rep captures the backyard on an iPad, on site. The space is measured where it stands, instead of being recreated later from guesswork.

02 · Design

Design on the actual lot.

The design sits on real property imagery, not a blank canvas. What the homeowner sees is their own yard, with the plan on top of it.

03 · Explore

Options that respect the rules.

AI assistance proposes layouts, and options are checked against setbacks and property boundaries, with measurement confidence visible on screen.

04 · Decide

Leave with a decision.

A branded proposal built on the contractor's own pricing, plus a dimensioned plan, in the homeowner's hands before the rep drives away.

Why it holds

Capture once, on site. Everything downstream stays true to it.

Capture, not recreate.

The real yard is captured the moment the rep is standing in it, so nothing has to be rebuilt from memory later.

Fewer errors, less rework.

Keeping the layout grounded in the real yard during the visit cuts guessed dimensions and redraws after the fact. Field verification still happens before construction.

One flow, lower cost.

Capture, design, collaboration, and proposal collapse into a single visit, which means less operational cost per opportunity.

Company

Built with purpose from day one.

Imaginova is a software company focused on transforming how outdoor environments are designed, visualized, and sold. We develop tools that combine spatial technology, artificial intelligence, and real-time visualization to help professionals work more effectively.

We founded the company in 2025 on a clear thesis: the outdoor living industry deserves purpose-built software that reflects how professionals actually work. Most design and sales tools used today were built for other industries and adapted imperfectly.

We are a small, focused team combining deep experience in software, sales technology, and spatial computing. We work with a select group of early industry partners to make sure what we build solves real problems in the real world.

Our approach is deliberate. Rather than building broadly and quickly, we are developing a production-grade platform that is reliable, easy to use in the field, and designed to scale across an organization of any size.

Imaginova, LLC is a registered limited liability company operating in the United States.
